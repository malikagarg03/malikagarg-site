Build My AI Learning Partner

Online courses make it easy to watch, understand and move on — but much harder to remember, connect and apply what you learn.

Tell AI who you are, what you are learning and why it matters to you. This tool will create a reusable instruction that turns lecture transcripts, slides and readings into a personalised learning conversation.

Note Personalisation is not telling AI your "learning style." It is giving it enough context to understand what you know, what you care about and what you want to become capable of doing.

When you start a new lecture

Once your learning-partner instruction is in place, your prompt for each lecture can stay simple.

Here are the transcript and slides for the next lecture. Let's work through it using my learning-partner instructions.
Before we begin this lecture, first test whether there is anything important from previous lectures that I should retrieve.
Why this works
Purpose

AI knows what you are ultimately trying to become capable of doing.

Connection

AI can relate new ideas to your experience and previous knowledge.

Active learning

AI can make you retrieve, explain, discriminate and apply rather than simply consume answers.
