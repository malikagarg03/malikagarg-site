Your context-setting prompt

Once you've read the AI's answer and have a sense of what the piece is about, generate the next prompt below — it'll quiz you on words from the same text before showing you any answers.

3 Test yourself, word by word

Guess before you're told

It turns the AI into a quizmaster instead of a dictionary: meaningful words and short phrases from the article, script plus transliteration, meaning held back until you've guessed, and one clean word list to copy out at the end. Best kept in the same chat as step 2, so the AI already has a sense of the context.

Your word-testing prompt

Go through all the rounds it gives you before moving on. Step 4 will take you into a full sentence-by-sentence read of the same passage.

4 Read it sentence by sentence

Attempt it, then check it

Keep this in the same chat as step 3 — the closing summary at the end depends on the AI remembering how your vocabulary quiz went. Same idea as step 3: you try first. For each sentence you'll be asked for your own attempt before the AI gives the real translation, a word-by-word (or chunk-by-chunk) breakdown, and the grammar behind it — plus a flag whenever something is a pattern you'll see again. At the end, it closes with a summary built from what you actually got wrong across steps 3 and 4, not a generic recap.

Your sentence-by-sentence prompt

Once you've read through every sentence, generate the last prompt below — it steps back from the language itself to the real issue the article touches on.

5 The issue behind the article

Go one level deeper

A short, one-shot close — no back-and-forth needed after four steps already, and no need to repaste the article since the AI already has it from earlier in this chat. It asks the AI to look past this one document to the real issue behind it, and what a police officer specifically should take from it — tied to your cadre if you entered one above.
