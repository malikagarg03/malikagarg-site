Avoid including sensitive operational details. You may use this exercise to examine any professional problem you choose.
The scenario

You have been given ₹5 crore under the Ministry of Home Affairs' Modernisation of Police Forces (MoPF) scheme. You have been asked to propose one project that would meaningfully improve policing.

What would you propose?

Alternatively, if your facilitator has agreed, you may work on a different professional problem or proposal you genuinely care about. Begin by recording your thinking in Section 1 — without using AI. The prompts will be revealed one stage at a time. Your responses are saved in this browser only.
